Letter

Pending Catastrophe

20 September 2021 — John Brice to Councillor David Wright & John Truman (Ballina Shire Council), cc Paul Busmanis, Teddi Russell, Shane Russell. Forwarded separately to Chris MacColl, Essential Energy.

David

I am writing to you as you are the head of Council. In that role you have a responsibility of office. I write to you as all under you defend their positions that are undefendable.

My investigations over the past 9 months have revealed.

  1. The Registered Council Drainage Easement under discussion was set up to handle "Large volumes of Storm water from the Airport and surrounds" to quickly drain to Emigrant Creek.
  2. The easement was overlaid on an existing "Farming Drain" which was draining the low lying blocks in question. You linked that by culverts to the airport to facilitate high volume drainage.
  3. The council had and has a duty of care to allow that drain to drain. Yes, it is at the councils discretion to drain.
  4. The council in its own admission has not maintained that Registered Drainage Easement for decades.
  5. The council allowed Essential Energy in 1986 to upgrade the poles which had been there since 1968. Those poles are in the Council Drainage Easement.
  6. With the upgrade of new poles, Essential Energy made a mistake and put a service road in to do the obvious. That service road blocks/dams three old farming drains and all natural run off. They are aware they can't put a road in to alter natural land contours.
  7. You and John confirmed at our meeting that you knew nothing of this Access Road until I mentioned it.
  8. That service road appears on the Ballina Council Plans site as being elevated above ground level. Somebody knew or knows about it. The three Monkey days are over.
  9. That service road since 1986 has acted as a dam, now trapping all natural and drain storm water run-off from the land bordered by Dead Man's Gully Road, Tamarind Drive, Summerhill Crescent and the Council Bike track.
  10. The low lying land is at 100% saturation, now with surface water ranging from 200mm to 800mm deep. We guess about 600 Olympic swimming pools of water are now trapped. All vegetation has been rotting at the rate of ground saturation over the past 35 years.
  11. That service road narrowed your easement from 10 metres to 3.
  12. Due to no maintenance your reduced easement is clogged with weeds metres tall. There is virtually no movement of water down it to Emigrant Creek. However there is water movement up Emigrant Creek facilitated by poor non-maintained one-way flaps on the Emigrant Creek culverts. The valve is or was frozen 150mm open. Paul Busmanis was notified of this by Michael Bald from Ardill Payne back when we met with him months ago.
  13. Paul Busmanis at the same meeting said he would commission experts to see if the council could even consider now clearing the remaining drain due to Black water concerns entering Emigrant Creek.
  14. Rome is burning.
  15. The problem is now bigger than all of us — recent photos taken of the Airport runway show water lapping on it due to not draining away.
  16. The next weather event will put the runway under water, as now "Large volumes of Storm water from the Airport and surrounds" can't drain at all.
  17. Essential Energy can't now mow half of the Access Road due to their road being at 100% ground water saturation. This road was not built as a dam. What happens if the dam breaks?
  18. It is absolutely critical council addresses drainage issues and puts the three Monkeys back on the shelf.
  19. David, at our last meeting with John Truman, you simply said it is my problem, take it up with Essential Energy.

I am now saying it is your problem, created by Ballina Council. Please address these issues as I am now holding you to your office mandates.

As a matter of interest I'm doing this because I actually do care. All I see is people running away from the truth and their responsibilities. David, I can still remember your first words to me when I spoke to you. You said "if what you say is correct I will fix it, but it is not just me, I must get the support from others". With not meaning to be disrespectful, it's time you got the support of others.

This will not go away. I will not go away.

John Brice
For and on behalf of the Land owners, Theodora Andrea Brice and Shane Russell.
