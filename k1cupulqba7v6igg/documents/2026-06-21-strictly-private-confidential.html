Strictly Private & Confidential — For Your Personal Attention Only

21 June 2026 — John Brice to Martin English, cc Teddi Russell
This letter accompanied two draft complaint documents (EPA and NSW Ombudsman) sent as separate attachments. Those documents are not yet uploaded to this record.

Dear Martin,

Please find attached for your private and personal attention two documents referenced in my formal correspondence to you this morning.

Attachment 1 — Updated Formal Complaint to the NSW Environment Protection Authority.
Attachment 2 — Formal Complaint to the NSW Ombudsman regarding the conduct of Essential Energy senior executives.

Both documents are substantially more detailed and forensically precise than anything you have previously seen in this matter. Both are provided to you privately and personally as General Counsel in the genuine hope that their contents inform your consideration of our family's counter offer before close of business tomorrow. Both remain held pending settlement.

Martin, I want to say something to you personally that goes beyond the formal correspondence.

Since your offer of 19th June, I have had time to reflect carefully. The $200,000 your organisation has offered is not a coincidence. I understand now that this has never truly been about the EPA obligations, the Class 2 Acid Sulphate Soils, the Category 2 Parkland classification, or 40 years of environmental damage to my daughter's land. It has been about a system designed to calculate the cost of a person's resistance and price them out of the fight.

I want you to know that system has met the wrong person.

I have spent five years seeking the truth. Your organisation has spent five years and considerable resources blocking it. I have done this without a legal team, without corporate resources, and without a single day of surrender — because this has always been about my daughter, her husband, and what is right.

You have underestimated me, Martin. You have taken honesty, truth, and caring as a weakness.

This is not personal. But it is also not going away. Not on my watch.

$1,200,000.00. Close of business tomorrow.

Kind regards,
John Brice
For and on behalf of the Property Owners Theodora Andrea Brice (Teddi) and Shane Russell
