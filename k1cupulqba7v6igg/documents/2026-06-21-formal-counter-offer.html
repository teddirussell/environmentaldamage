Dear Martin and Amalie

Thank you for Essential Energy's response of 19 June 2026. I have reviewed your offer carefully and held a further family meeting over the weekend. I respond as follows.

Essential Energy's offer of $200,000 is respectfully rejected.

I have however read your three conditions carefully and I respond to each directly and in complete good faith.

Condition 1 — the existing access track remaining in situ. Accepted without reservation. Our family has never sought the physical removal of the access road as part of this settlement.

Condition 2 — the grant of a suitable easement securing ongoing access. Accepted. We are prepared to grant Essential Energy a formal registered easement securing permanent ongoing access, strictly within the boundaries of the existing registered Ballina Shire Council Drainage Easement established in 1984. In recognition of the fact that Essential Energy has occupied and operated within this easement without lawful authority since 1986 — a period of 40 years — our family attributes a separate commercial consideration of $200,000 to the formal legal legitimisation of that occupation.

Condition 3 — a Deed of Release on terms satisfactory to Essential Energy. Accepted. We remain fully committed to executing a comprehensive Deed of Release granting Essential Energy, its executives, and its contractors total immunity from any past, present, or future civil claims or liabilities regarding the access road, soil conditions, and drainage matters.

Our formal counter offer:

  • Component 1 — Commercial Settlement: $1,000,000.00
  • Component 2 — Registered Easement Grant (separate consideration for 40 years of unauthorised occupation): $200,000.00
Total Settlement Payment: $1,200,000.00
